Terms

Booking and payment

This page takes a request, not a booking. You send the details, we check the date with the operator and quote you on WhatsApp. Nothing is held until you accept the quote.

Payment is 100% at booking. Once you accept the quote we send a payment link or bank and QR details. The charter is confirmed when payment is received. No card details are taken on this page.

Weather

Sea conditions are decided by the operator and by the authorities. If the charter cannot sail on safety grounds, we substitute another date, or another vessel or route of the same standard, by agreement with you. Where no substitution works, refunds follow the operator's policy below.

Cancellation and refunds

Cancellation and refund terms are those of the operator who runs the charter. They differ by vessel and by season, and we set them out in writing in your quote before you pay. Refunds are made per that operator policy. Amounts already paid to the operator on your behalf are refundable only to the extent the operator refunds them.

Our role

Eternis Tours arranges these charters with licensed operators; the operator's own conditions of carriage apply. You travel under the operator's conditions, and the operator is responsible for the vessel, the crew and the conduct of the day.
